Who this is for

You were injured in an Ontario accident

Car, motorcycle, pedestrian, cyclist, transit, or rideshare — accident benefits may apply regardless of fault.

Insurance has delayed, denied, or reduced benefits

A plain-language review can help you understand your options if a decision feels unfair or stalled.

You're unsure what benefits apply

Ontario's Statutory Accident Benefits Schedule (SABS) is complex. We help you make sense of it without jargon.

You're missing work or paying out-of-pocket

Income replacement, treatment, and rehabilitation coverage may be available. We'll help you understand what you can claim.

Your claim feels confusing or stalled

Sometimes the next step is just clarity — knowing what you're entitled to ask for, and when.
